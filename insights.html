Bihar CSR Outlook Report 2026

The first district-by-district study of CSR in Bihar. It covers four years of official MCA data, how Bihar compares with India's largest states, seven development challenges, and a partnership framework for companies and government.

Inside the report

What you will learn

Section 1

Bihar's CSR landscape

National comparisons, a four-year trend with an outlook to FY 2025-26, flows to all 38 districts and who funds them.

Section 2

Seven development challenges

Nutrition, learning, health, jobs, floods, women's enterprise and water, each with a ready-to-fund programme.

Section 3

Partnering for Bihar

Recommendations, a State-Orvys partnership framework and 2030 targets.
