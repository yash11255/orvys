Data and research

CSR figures on this website come from public sources, mainly Ministry of Corporate Affairs data based on companies' MCA21 filings, as reported in replies to questions in Parliament. Development indicators come from sources such as the National Family Health Survey and the Census of India. These sources may be revised over time.

Figures marked as estimates, projections or targets, such as CSR outlooks for FY 2024-25 and FY 2025-26 or 2030 targets, are Orvys's own indicative calculations, not official data.

General information only

Articles about CSR law and compliance are for general information and are not legal, tax or financial advice. Rules change; always check the latest notifications from the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and consult a qualified professional.

Programme budgets

Programme models and budgets on this website are indicative. Actual costs depend on location, scope and implementing partners, and are agreed separately with each partner.
