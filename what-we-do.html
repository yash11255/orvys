What we do

One accountable partner across the full CSR lifecycle: strategy, design, delivery and proof of impact.

01

CSR strategy & data

CSR policy, annual action plans and Schedule VII alignment, guided by district-level data on needs and existing CSR flows.

→ 02

Programme design

Investment-ready programmes with a theory of change, baseline, budget, outcome indicators and government endorsement.

→ 03

Partner due diligence

Field-verified, CSR-1-registered NGOs with checks on governance, finances and track record.

→ 04

Implementation & monitoring

Programme management, field visits, monthly reporting and a live dashboard that tracks every rupee and milestone.

→ 05

Impact assessment

Independent baseline, mid-line and endline studies, with impact reports ready for your board and annual report.

→ 06

Government partnerships

State CSR facilitation cells, project banks and convergence with flagship schemes.

→ 07

Research & insights

State CSR outlook reports, sector studies and custom analysis that shape where India's CSR goes next.

Geography intelligence

Business alignment

Which causes fit your sector, your brand and your people?

Community voice
