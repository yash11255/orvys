What is Bihar CSR?

CSR in Bihar, explained

"Bihar CSR" refers to Corporate Social Responsibility spending by companies in the state of Bihar under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013. Companies above certain profit, turnover or net worth thresholds must spend 2% of their average net profit on approved social activities.

CSR in Bihar has grown quickly, from ₹89.89 crore in FY 2020-21 to ₹260.53 crore in FY 2023-24. Yet Bihar, home to about 1 in 11 Indians, still receives only about 1% of the CSR that can be traced to a state. Private companies gave 65% of Bihar's CSR in FY 2023-24 and PSUs gave 35%.

District-wise CSR in Bihar

Where CSR goes inside Bihar

CSR in Bihar is highly concentrated. In FY 2023-24, Muzaffarpur (₹52.00 Cr) and Patna (₹38.72 Cr) received about 35% of the total, while 12 of 38 districts received under ₹1 Cr each. Arwal and Sheikhpura received none.

Bihar CSR FAQs

Frequently asked questions about CSR in Bihar

How much CSR does Bihar receive?

Companies spent ₹260.53 crore on CSR in Bihar in FY 2023-24, up from ₹89.89 crore in FY 2020-21, according to Ministry of Corporate Affairs data reported in Parliament. That is about 1% of India's state-attributed CSR.

Why does Bihar receive so little CSR?

CSR law asks companies to prefer the local areas where they operate. Bihar has relatively few large industrial bases, so fewer companies have a natural local mandate there. A shortage of investment-ready projects and perceived execution risk also play a part.

Which districts of Bihar receive the most CSR?

In FY 2023-24, Muzaffarpur (₹52.00 crore) and Patna (₹38.72 crore) received the most, followed by Begusarai, Bhojpur, Gaya and Bhagalpur.

What are the best CSR projects to fund in Bihar?

Child nutrition, foundational learning, primary healthcare, skilling with job placement, flood resilience, women's enterprise and safe drinking water are the areas where Bihar's needs are greatest and CSR can achieve the most.

Is CSR spending in Bihar eligible under Section 135?

Yes. CSR spending anywhere in India counts toward a company's obligation, provided the activity falls under Schedule VII and is carried out directly or through an eligible implementing agency.
