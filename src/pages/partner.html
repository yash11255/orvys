Questions

Frequently asked

Is a programme with Orvys eligible as CSR under Section 135?

Yes. Every programme is designed to fit Schedule VII activities and is delivered through CSR-1-registered implementing agencies, with documentation that supports your CSR-2 reporting.

Can we use our unspent CSR funds?

Yes. We design multi-year "ongoing projects" that can use funds held in your Unspent CSR Account, with timelines and milestones that meet the rules.

Do you work outside Bihar?

Yes. Bihar is our first focus state, but our model applies across India. We can design programmes in any geography that matches your priorities.

How do you measure impact?

Every programme has a baseline, outcome indicators, monthly monitoring and an independent endline study. You receive a live dashboard and a final impact report.

What is the minimum commitment?

Programme partnerships start at about ₹50 lakh a year. Smaller budgets can join pooled programmes alongside other companies.
