Why we exist

India spends over ₹40,000 crore a year on CSR. Where it goes decides who it helps.

Most CSR stays close to company headquarters. The places with the greatest need, and the greatest potential for impact, are left behind. Orvys exists to close that gap with evidence, strong partnerships and delivery you can measure.
